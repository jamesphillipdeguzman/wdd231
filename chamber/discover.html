Iloilo Overview

  • Population: ~2.5 million
  • Languages: Hiligaynon, Kinaray-a, English, Filipino
  • Literacy Rate: ~95%
  • Key Drivers: Agriculture, Trade, IT-BPO, Tourism, Manufacturing
  • Accolade: UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy

Historic Title: Bestowed as the Queen City of the South by royal decree in 1896 for its thriving international sugar trade.

Port of Iloilo: Opened to foreign merchant ships in 1855, establishing direct global commerce with Europe, the US, and Australia.

Educational Hub: Centers premier universities including University of the Philippines Visayas, Central Philippine University, and University of San Agustin.

Infrastructure: Features Iloilo International Airport in Cabatuan, modern seaport terminals, and a Panay-wide road network connecting antique, Capiz, and Aklan.
